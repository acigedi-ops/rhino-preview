The basics

What commercial auto covers, in plain English

Required in WA

Liability

Injuries and damage your drivers cause. A $1M combined single limit is the usual floor for contracts and DOT filings.

Often missed

Hired and non-owned auto

Covers rented vehicles and employees' personal cars used for work. Without it, a personal-policy denial can land on your business.

Your vehicles

Collision

Repairs your vehicles after a crash, whoever was at fault. Lenders and lessors usually require it.

Your vehicles

Comprehensive

Theft, vandalism, hail and fire, which matters for trucks parked overnight on job sites.

Must be offered in WA

Uninsured / underinsured motorist

Protects your drivers when the other driver has little or no insurance.

No-fault

Medical payments / PIP

Pays medical bills for your drivers and passengers regardless of fault.

Filings

DOT filings (MCS-90, BMC-91)

Federal and state filings for regulated carriers, kept current with FMCSA and Washington.

Equipment

Trailers and mobile equipment

Owned and rented trailers, compressors, generators and attachments. Cargo is a separate policy, and we'll tell you if you need it.

Questions

Commercial Auto questions

Can I use my personal auto policy for my business vehicle?

Usually not. Most personal policies exclude business use, which can leave you without coverage after a crash.

What is hired and non-owned auto?

Coverage for vehicles your business rents or that employees drive for work in their own cars.

Do I need an MCS-90?

If you are a for-hire motor carrier crossing state lines, or haul certain hazardous materials, usually yes. We'll check your operation.
