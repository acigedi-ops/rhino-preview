The basics

What builder's risk covers, in plain English

The project

Structure under construction

Fire, wind, theft, vandalism and most sudden accidental damage to the building in progress. Set the limit to the completed value.

On site

Materials

Lumber, fixtures and supplies staged on site, against theft and damage.

On the road

Materials in transit

Deliveries on your truck, a vendor's truck or a common carrier, under a separate sub-limit.

Site

Temporary structures

Fencing, scaffolding, forms and job trailers.

Weather

Wind, hail and storms

Standard weather perils. Flood and earthquake are usually excluded and placed separately.

Optional

Soft costs

Loan interest, taxes, permit fees and lost rent when a covered loss delays the project.

After a loss

Debris removal

Clearing the site after a covered loss, often under its own sub-limit.

Term

Length and renewals

Terms of 3, 6, 9 or 12 months, with extensions if the schedule slips. Coverage usually ends at occupancy, completion or sale.

Questions

Builder's Risk questions

Who buys builder's risk, the owner or the contractor?

Either one. The contract usually says who is responsible. We can write it in the name of whoever needs it and add the other as an insured.

My GC has general liability. Do I still need builder's risk?

Yes. General liability covers injuries and damage to others. Builder's risk covers the building itself while it's under construction.

What if my project runs long?

We extend the policy. Let us know before it expires so there's no gap.
