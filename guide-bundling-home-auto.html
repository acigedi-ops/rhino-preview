Does bundling home and auto actually save money?

When a bundle helps, when it doesn't, and how we check both ways.

By Rhino Insurance Group · Published October 11, 2026 · Updated October 22, 2026 · General information, not legal advice

Insurance ads promise big savings for bundling. Sometimes that's true, and sometimes two separate policies cost less. Here's how bundling works, when it helps, and how we check both ways for you.

Usually, yes

Most carriers give a multi-policy discount when your home or renters policy and your auto are written together. The discount can apply to both policies, and one renewal date and one company make life simpler.

But not always

Sometimes one carrier has the best home price and another has the best auto price, and the two separate policies cost less than any bundle. That's common for drivers with a ticket or an SR-22, or for older homes.

Because we compare several carriers, we price it both ways, bundled and separate, and show you the difference.

What can be bundled

  • Home, condo or renters with auto.
  • Several cars and drivers on one auto policy.
  • An umbrella policy on top of home and auto, which some carriers require to be with them.
  • Landlord policies for a rental property, with some carriers.

Other benefits of one carrier

Some carriers offer a single deductible when one event damages both your home and your car, like a tree falling on both. Having everything in one place can also make an umbrella policy easier to get.

The trade-offs

With everything at one carrier, a rate increase or a non-renewal on one policy can affect the other. If one policy has to move, the discount on the other may drop too. That's one more reason to have an agent who can re-shop both.

Renters count too

Renters insurance is inexpensive and can earn the same multi-policy discount on your car, sometimes saving more than the renters policy costs.

How we check

  1. We quote your home and auto separately with several carriers.
  2. We quote the bundles those carriers offer.
  3. We show you the best bundle next to the best separate pair, with the same coverage, so the comparison is fair.
